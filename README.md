# The Shattering

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | W A S D or arrow keys | Left stick or D-pad |
| Roll | Tap Space | Tap B |
| Sprint | Hold Space while moving | Hold B while moving |
| Jump | F | A |
| Light attack | Left mouse button or J | RB |
| Heavy attack | Right mouse button or K (hold to charge) | RT (hold to charge) |
| Block | Hold Shift or L | Hold LB |
| Parry | E | LT |
| Lock-on | Middle mouse button or Q (press again to let go) | R3 |
| Switch Weapon | Tab | Y |
| Spell | C (tap to cast the selected Spell: Pull drags an enemy or object closer or takes a torch's or a Fire's flame, Shove pushes an enemy or object away or throws the held flame, and Deep bolt fires a bolt along the aim) | L3 (press the left stick; tap to cast the selected Spell) |
| Switch Spell | V (cycles Pull, Shove and Deep bolt) | D-pad up |
| Sip from the Health Flask | 1 | View (Back) |
| Sip from the Mana Flask (once Almos has a Mana bar) | 2 | Menu (Start) |
| Interact | R (tap to rest at a Checkpoint, hide in or leave a Hiding spot, or pick up a Weapon; hold to drop the Weapon in hand) | X (tap to rest, hide, leave or pick up; hold to drop) |

- A Heavy attack pressed in the air is a Jump attack.
- A Light attack pressed next to a broken enemy is a Critical hit.
- A tap of Interact rests at a Checkpoint in reach, or else gets into a Hiding spot in reach, or else picks up a Weapon in reach; in a Hiding spot a tap leaves it, and Almos cannot move or act inside. An enemy that sees him get in drags him out with an Unblockable attack: tap Interact to leave during its wind-up and Roll away. Holding it drops the Weapon in hand where Almos stands. Almos carries every Weapon he picks up, and Switch Weapon cycles through them in the order they were picked up. A switch pressed in an attack waits for it to end, and is lost in a Guard break or a Parry. With no Weapon left in hand he fights Unarmed.
- Gamepad buttons are named as on an Xbox pad: A is the bottom face button, B the right one.
