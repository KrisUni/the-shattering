# The Broken World

A top-down 2D action RPG with souls-lite combat, set on a world that stopped spinning: one half in permanent night, one in permanent day, the Tempest between them.

## Language

### World

**Marked**:
A Nar born to the Path of the Deep, required to wear a Seal. Almos is Marked.
_Avoid_: Marked one, Deep mage

**Seal**:
The ring the Marked wear around the neck, which stops them channelling. Being an unsealed Marked is a crime among the Nar. At the end of the Base of the tree, an Ilion latches onto Almos's Seal and drains it until it breaks.
_Avoid_: Ring, neck ring, collar

**Ilion**:
A parasite that leeches magical energy from the arch tree, and from Nar mages when it can reach them. It grows as Small Ilion and Large Ilion. Touching any Ilion hurts, so striking one in melee hurts Almos too. Emrys drains Ilion to restore himself.
_Avoid_: Ilion plant, mage-eater

### Areas

In play order: Base of the tree → Dungeons → Upper tree or Catacombs → Lands between → Tempest. The areas are fully connected: the player can return to any area, including the arch tree, at any time.

**Open map**:
An area laid out as one continuous space that the camera follows Almos through. Open maps join each other through doorways or paths with a short load.
_Avoid_: Overworld, level, zone

**Room**:
A single screen-sized space inside a building or the Dungeons. Leaving through an edge or door moves the view to the next Room. Smaller buildings within an Open map, and the whole of the Dungeons, are built from Rooms.
_Avoid_: Screen, chamber

**Base of the tree**:
The first area, at the foot of the arch tree, where the game begins. It opens with an errand: Emrys, already dying, sends Almos down through the lower tree to fetch Ilion, which he says slows his sickness, and the errand teaches movement and combat. Its enemies are Ilion and other creatures that have crept in from the dark. The last stretch begins back in Emrys's room with Emrys and Massel: the Ilion from Almos's Ilion jar latches onto his Seal and drains it until it breaks, Massel calls the guards, and Emrys drains that Ilion to restore himself and escapes. The Nar guards turn on Almos, and the area ends with his Capture.
_Avoid_: Tree base, roots

**Ilion jar**:
The sealed jar Emrys gives Almos at the start of the errand. It holds one Ilion, gathered without harm; any other Ilion on the way he must fight or avoid. Handing the filled jar back to Emrys completes the errand. When Emrys opens it, the Ilion inside latches onto Almos's Seal and breaks it.
_Avoid_: Jar, specimen jar, container

**Capture**:
How the Base of the tree ends. If the Nar guards bring Almos's Health to zero, or he reaches the dead end of the last stretch, they take him to the Dungeons. It is not a death: no Remains are left and he keeps his Experience.
_Avoid_: Arrest, forced death

**Dungeons**:
The prison in the roots beneath the arch tree, where Almos is held after his Seal breaks. It is built entirely from Rooms. Almos wakes in a cell of living roots and gets out by burning through it with his first Flame draw. He enters stripped of his Weapon and Armour, keeping only his Flasks, and fights Unarmed, with improvised Weapons and with Flame draw. Its enemies are Nar jailers, who Block and Parry, and creatures living in the roots. He meets no NPCs here.
_Avoid_: Prison, cells, roots

**Store room**:
A guarded Room deep in the Dungeons, off the direct route out, where Almos's confiscated Weapon and Armour are kept. Recovering them is optional. His gear at this point is modest; nothing in it is precious.
_Avoid_: Armoury, evidence room, stash

**Upper tree**:
The trunk and canopy of the arch tree, reached from the Dungeons by a guarded path. Almos leaves it by climbing down the Great root. The area of Jumps and Deadly falls. Its enemies are Nar guards hunting the fugitive, some attacking from higher branches, and creatures of the canopy. He meets no NPCs here.
_Avoid_: Canopy, treetop, branches

**Great root**:
A huge root that arches from the arch tree's canopy down to the ground beyond its gates, linking the Upper tree to the Lands between.
_Avoid_: Vine, outer root, ladder

**Catacombs**:
A hidden series of dark corridors leading from the Dungeons underground to the Lands between: an alternative to the Upper tree, not a shortcut, and about as long. Nothing later in the game depends on which route the player took. They are old Nar burial chambers in near-total darkness, lined with resting Undead, so the player must balance each torch between light to see by and a fireball to fight with.
_Avoid_: Tunnels, crypts, sewers

**Lands between**:
The ground beyond the arch tree that Almos reaches after his escape, where he meets the Time Keeper and fights the Lesser god. Its enemies are wild creatures of the dark and the Undead.
_Avoid_: Wilds, outlands, midlands

**Tempest**:
The storm-wracked band between the dayside and the Shadowlands; the last of the first areas. Its enemies are storm-hardened creatures and hostile mages, Necro mages among them. Its Wind pushes the player, and Lightning strikes the ground. On Almos's first arrival a Storm peak builds as he goes deeper, and Storm exposure drives him to look for cover; searching for it leads him to an underground tunnel that ends at Mani and Cersei's Shelter. Meeting them there is a story moment, not an ending: the first areas have no closing scene and simply stop where their content runs out.
_Avoid_: Twilight zone, storm belt

**Vision**:
The dreamlike place in the Lands between that Almos enters by touching a field of glowing fungus, where he fights the Lesser god. Dying there leaves his Remains at the entrance in the real world.
_Avoid_: Dream, trip, hallucination

**Fugitive**:
Almos's standing in the arch tree after his escape. From then on, Nar guards patrol the Base of the tree, the Dungeons and the Upper tree alongside the creatures, and treat him as an enemy on sight.
_Avoid_: Wanted, outlaw, exile

**Wind**:
The Tempest's gusts, which push the player in a visible direction. Rolls, Jumps and Sprints carry further with the Wind and fall short against it.
_Avoid_: Gust, storm push

**Storm cycle**:
The Tempest's storm rising and falling over time. The ordinary cycle makes the Wind and Lightning stronger and weaker, so the Tempest is generally playable; only rarely does it reach a Storm peak.
_Avoid_: Weather cycle, storm timer

**Storm peak**:
The height of the storm's intensity, during which it deals Storm exposure. The Storm cycle reaches one only rarely and always with a long warning, such as the sky darkening and the Wind howling. Almos's first arrival in the Tempest is a Storm peak, as are certain special events.
_Avoid_: Storm surge, superstorm

**Storm exposure**:
The steady damage the storm deals during a Storm peak to anyone not behind real cover, such as an overhang, a ruin or a large rock, that blocks the Wind.
_Avoid_: Storm damage, exposure, weather damage

**Shelter**:
Mani and Cersei's home beneath the Tempest, reached through a small underground tunnel of their own that Almos finds while looking for cover from Storm exposure. It is not connected to the great tunnel beneath the Tempest.
_Avoid_: Hideout, home, refuge

**Lightning**:
A Tempest strike marked on the ground a moment before it lands. Parrying it lets the player catch it as a Storm draw.
_Avoid_: Bolt, thunder strike

### Player state

**Health**, **Stamina**, **Mana**:
The player's three bars. Only Stamina refills by itself; Health and Mana come back only at Checkpoints and from Flasks. Almos has no Mana bar at first: he gains it from the Time Keeper early in the Lands between.
_Avoid_: HP, energy, FP, mana points

**Shortcut**:
A way the player opens that links a deeper part of an area back to a Checkpoint, such as a burnt door or a dropped ladder. Once opened, it stays open for good.
_Avoid_: Unlock, back door

**Health Flask**, **Mana Flask**:
The two refillable vessels the player carries, each holding its own fixed number of charges that restore its bar. Every Checkpoint refills both, and upgrades found while exploring add charges. Almos carries the Mana Flask from the start, but it stays empty and useless until he gains his Mana bar.
_Avoid_: Estus, potion bottle, potions (as separate items)

**Sip**:
Drinking one Flask charge: a short animation that cannot be cancelled, during which the player can only walk slowly. The bar refills at the end; a hit before then wastes the charge.
_Avoid_: Drink, heal, quaff

**Flask upgrade**:
A collectible found while exploring that adds one charge to both the Health Flask and the Mana Flask.
_Avoid_: Estus shard, flask shard, charge upgrade

**Experience**:
What the player gains by killing enemies; each kind of enemy gives its own amount. It only turns into Levels when cashed in at a Checkpoint; Experience not yet cashed in is at risk when the player dies. It is not a currency and cannot be spent anywhere else.
_Avoid_: Souls, XP, currency, runes

**Level**:
One step of the player's growth, bought with Experience at a Checkpoint. Each Level adds one point to a stat of the player's choice: Health, Stamina, Mana, Strength or Channelling. Mana can be levelled before Almos has a Mana bar; the points are waiting in it when he gains it.
_Avoid_: Level-up point, rank

**Strength**:
The stat that raises Weapon damage and sets the greatest Weight a Spell can move.
_Avoid_: Might, power, attack

**Weight**:
How heavy an enemy or object is. A Spell that pushes or lifts can only move things whose Weight is within what the player's Strength allows.
_Avoid_: Mass, size class, poise

**Channelling**:
The stat that raises Spell damage.
_Avoid_: Intelligence, magic, arcane, faith

**Remains**:
The marker left where the player died, holding all the Experience not yet cashed in; after a Deadly fall it is left on the last solid ground the player stood on, and after a death in the Vision it is left at the Vision's entrance. The player recovers that Experience by touching it, with no button. Dying again before reaching it destroys it and leaves new Remains, or none if the player had no Experience then; there is only ever one set. Resting leaves it where it is.
_Avoid_: Bloodstain, corpse, grave, souls

**Checkpoint**:
A resting place that refills all three bars and both Flasks and becomes the player's return point. In the world, each Checkpoint is an arch-tree shoot: a small glowing sapling grown from the arch tree's roots, common in and around the tree and rarer the further Almos travels. Each area has about two: one near where the player enters and one before its hardest stretch, with Shortcuts that link the rest of the area back to them. One more stands beside the Vision's entrance. It is also where Experience is cashed in for Levels and where Armour is changed. The player rests at one with a tap of Interact, within reach of it and while free to act; when a Checkpoint, a Hiding spot and a Weapon are all in reach, the tap rests at the Checkpoint before it hides in the Hiding spot, and hides before it picks the Weapon up. Resting is instant, and it brings back every ordinary enemy while Bosses and named enemies stay dead. Resting is not sleep: it works the same after the Lesser god takes Almos's ability to sleep.
_Avoid_: Bonfire, save point, shrine

**Death**:
What happens when the player's Health reaches zero. Almos goes limp where he fell, taking no input and unable to be hit, and every action he was in ends. After a pause he returns to his last Checkpoint with full bars and every ordinary enemy back, as after a rest, while Bosses and named enemies stay dead. The Experience he carried is left as Remains where he fell.
_Avoid_: Game over, respawn

### Movement and combat

**Lock-on**:
The state in which the player targets a single enemy; while it holds, movement circles that enemy and every attack and roll is aimed relative to it. Holding a direction unchanged keeps him circling the same way; a new direction is read afresh against the line to the target.
_Avoid_: Target lock, Z-targeting, focus

**Weapon**:
Anything the player can sensibly fight with, each with its own Light attack and Heavy attack: a sword, spear or greatsword, but also an improvised object such as a torch or a metal bar. Almos carries his Weapons in the order he picked them up. One button switches to the next at once, and a switch pressed during an attack waits for it to end. A tap of Interact picks one up, and a hold drops the one in hand where he stands, with the next carried Weapon coming to hand. Every carried Weapon counts toward the player's Load. They never break and are never upgraded; those found deeper in the world are stronger.
_Avoid_: Blade, arm, gear

**Armour**:
A single piece of protective gear found in the world that reduces damage taken, at the cost of a slower, shorter Roll as it gets heavier. The player wears one piece at a time and changes it only at a Checkpoint.
_Avoid_: Gear, outfit, equipment (for armour alone)

**Load**:
The total Weight of everything the player wears and carries: Armour and every Weapon. While it stays within the limit set by the Stamina stat, the Roll is normal; past it, the Roll becomes slower and shorter; past a hard ceiling above that, the player can pick up nothing more.
_Avoid_: Equip load, burden, encumbrance

**Unarmed**:
What Almos fights with when his hands are empty: weak Light and Heavy attacks, with Stagger and Parry still working. It is not a stop when switching between Weapons. Dropping the last Weapon leaves him Unarmed.
_Avoid_: Fists, bare hands, brawling

**Light attack**:
A quick swing that chains into a short combo; costs stamina and commits the player to its animation.
_Avoid_: Attack, slash, quick attack

**Heavy attack**:
A slower, harder swing that can be charged by holding and builds more Stagger than a Light attack; its Stagger grows with the charge as its damage does. A tap shorter than the charge start gives the plain Heavy attack; a fully charged one comes out by itself.
_Avoid_: Strong attack, power attack, charge attack

**Spell**:
A channelled ability with its own button. Spells normally cost Mana and Stamina, since channelling is physical work. Almos's first Spell is Flame draw; Shove, Pull and Deep bolt come from the Time Keeper's lessons in channelling.
_Avoid_: Magic, ability, skill, cast

**Flame draw**:
Almos's first Spell, discovered in the Dungeons: he channels the fire out of a magical torch and throws it as a single fireball. It costs Health and Stamina instead of Mana and Stamina, and each torch's flame can be drawn only once. That includes a torch carried as a Weapon, which lights the way while it burns and is left as an unlit club once drawn. Torches on walls and stands relight whenever enemies return.
_Avoid_: Torch spell, fire grab, flame weapon

**Shove**:
A Spell that pushes an enemy or object away from the player. It can only move things whose Weight is within what Strength allows.
_Avoid_: Push, force push, telekinesis

**Pull**:
A Spell that drags an enemy or object toward the player, including items across gaps the player cannot Jump. Limited by Weight, like Shove.
_Avoid_: Grab, yank, telekinesis

**Deep bolt**:
A ranged Spell of the Path of the Deep, paid for with Mana and Stamina; the player's everyday ranged attack, needing no torch or storm.
_Avoid_: Magic missile, mana bolt, blast

**Storm draw**:
Catching a Parried Lightning strike and throwing it as a single bolt, in the manner of Flame draw. It costs Stamina only, since the storm supplies the power. A caught bolt lasts only a few seconds before it fades.
_Avoid_: Lightning catch, bolt throw, thunder parry

**Fire**:
Burning started by a fireball, or by touching a lit torch in hand to something flammable. Anything flammable, such as living roots, barrels, curtains, wooden doors or dry fungus, catches and spreads it to flammable things nearby. Fire hurts anyone standing in it, Almos included, and a burning Hiding spot forces him out. Burnt Hiding spots and fungus come back whenever enemies return; anything burnt that blocked a path stays open for good.
_Avoid_: Flames, burning, blaze

**Roll**:
The player's preferred defence: a quick evasive move in any direction that costs stamina and briefly makes the player untouchable.
_Avoid_: Dodge, dash, evade

**Sprint**:
Fast running, done by holding the Roll button while moving; drains Stamina continuously and stops it refilling while it lasts. Running out of Stamina ends a Sprint until the button is released and held again.
_Avoid_: Run, dash

**Jump**:
A hop with its own button that carries the player across gaps, up onto low ledges, and over Low attacks. It gives no invulnerability: anything else still hits, and a shadow under the player shows his height. An Unblockable attack is avoided only by carrying him out of its reach. It costs Stamina, which may be none; with too little to pay for it there is no Jump. The player keeps the speed and direction he had as he took off, to the end, with no steering: walking, Sprinting or blocking carry him as far as that speed goes, and standing still jumps in place. It can be started from a walk, a Sprint (which it ends) or a Block, but not in a Roll, an attack, a Parry or a Guard break, where the press is lost. In the air the player can neither Roll, Block nor Parry, and those presses are lost too, as is a Light attack press; a Heavy attack press makes a Jump attack.
There are two height levels, the ground and the low ledge. On the ground, gaps and the sides of low ledges block the player like walls, and enemies as well; in the air only real walls do, and enemies are passed over. Where the player comes down sets his level: inside a ledge puts him on it, where its own edges keep him in, and anywhere else is the ground. Coming down on an enemy does not stand him on it: he is pushed out sideways. Coming down inside a gap is a Deadly fall; until that is built it puts the player back where the Jump began.
_Avoid_: Leap, hop, vault

**Jump attack**:
A Heavy attack started in mid-air that comes down hard and builds extra Stagger, more than any Heavy attack. The press can come on any frame of the Jump after take-off and begins it at once: nothing is charged, it costs Stamina when it begins, and with none left the press is lost and the Jump goes on. The player keeps flying the rest of the Jump unchanged, and the strike lands on the frame he comes down, in the direction he was aiming at the press: at the Lock-on target under Lock-on, the way he faces otherwise. He is committed from the press to the end of the recovery, so he cannot Roll, Jump, Block or Parry; a Jump attack that hits nothing still takes its full recovery. It reaches the enemy like any swing, so an attack from behind adds its flat Stagger as usual. It is not a Critical hit and restarts the Light combo. A Jump that comes down in a gap puts him back where it began and cancels the Jump attack with it, with no strike and no recovery.
_Avoid_: Plunge, aerial, dive attack

**Drop**:
Stepping or jumping off an edge onto ground below. A Drop shorter than the safe height is harmless; one past it, or into empty space, is a Deadly fall.
_Avoid_: Fall (for a safe drop), ledge hop

**Deadly fall**:
A Drop past the safe height or into empty space, which kills the player. Nothing warns of it in advance; players learn which edges are deadly by dying.
_Avoid_: Pit, fall damage, void-out

**Block**:
A held guard that reduces incoming damage without negating it; each blocked hit also costs Stamina. It covers only the front, and with no Stamina left it protects nothing. While blocking, the player walks slowly, cannot Sprint, and refills Stamina more slowly. A Roll or an attack can be started from it, and a Block pressed during either waits for it to end. The fallback when a Roll isn't possible.
_Avoid_: Guard, shield

**Guard break**:
The helpless moment that follows when a blocked hit takes the player's last Stamina, leaving them open to a follow-up. The hit that causes it is still reduced. For a set time the player stands still and cannot Roll, attack or Block; presses are lost, and every hit lands in full. Lock-on keeps working. It lasts long enough for an enemy already in range to swing again into it; one that must first close in may come too late. That holds for attacks Block could have stopped; it does not cover an Unblockable attack, whose long wind-up is meant to be escaped by a Roll, so a Guard break may be over before it strikes.
_Avoid_: Shield break, block break

**Parry**:
A tight-timing defence that negates an incoming attack and adds to the attacker's Stagger; the hardest and most precise defence. A press opens a short window; a hit from the front inside it deals no damage, never reaches Block, and ends the Parry at once. It is judged from the front the same way Block's is, costs no Stamina and works at zero Stamina. A window that catches nothing leaves the player standing still through a recovery: they cannot Roll, attack, Block or Parry, and every hit lands in full. It can be started from a Sprint or while blocking, which it stops (Block comes back afterwards if still held); a press during a Roll, an attack, a Heavy attack charge, a Guard break or another Parry is lost, not kept, so presses never stack, and a Parry never overlaps untouchable frames. Lock-on keeps working through it. The attacker is told of a Parried hit, for Stagger.
_Avoid_: Deflect, counter

**Stagger**:
An enemy's accumulated loss of balance, shown as a bar over the enemy that fills toward a Break. Parries build it most; Heavy attacks and Jump attacks build more slowly, Heavy attacks more the more they are charged; Light attacks build none from the front. Any hit from behind adds a flat amount on top, so a Light attack from behind builds a little. A Critical hit builds none. Any landed hit restarts a pause, and once the pause is over the meter drains. It is separate from Health and never takes any.
_Avoid_: Poise, posture, balance

**Break**:
The moment an enemy's Stagger fills. It interrupts the enemy's attack at once, empties the meter, and builds no Stagger for as long as it lasts, though hits still take Health. The enemy stands still: it walks, turns and attacks no more. It ends when its time runs out or a Critical hit lands.
_Avoid_: Stun, posture break

**Critical hit**:
Almos's own move on a broken enemy: a Light attack press made close enough to the enemy's centre makes it in place of a Light swing, at the nearest broken enemy in range, aimed at its true angle. Under Lock-on to another enemy he still turns to face the broken one for the whole swing; the lock holds and he faces its target again once the swing is over. It deals heavy damage, ends the Break, costs no Stamina (so it works at zero Stamina), builds no Stagger and restarts the Light combo. Two gaps are accepted: another enemy caught in its swing also takes its damage, and a Critical hit that lands after the Break has run out deals its damage but has nothing to end.
_Avoid_: Riposte, deathblow, visceral

### People

**NPC**:
A character the player can talk to, placed as an anchor for the story; some hand Almos an item at a story moment. NPCs give no quests and do not trade.
_Avoid_: Villager, quest giver, merchant

**Emrys**:
Almos's Guardian, dying, met in the Base of the tree. When an Ilion breaks Almos's Seal, Emrys drains that Ilion to restore himself and escapes the arch tree alone. He does not appear in the Dungeons or the Upper tree.
_Avoid_: The Guardian, mentor

**Massel**:
A young Marked child in the Base of the tree, on whom Emrys draws to stay alive. When Almos's Seal breaks, Massel calls the guards.
_Avoid_: The child, the Marked child

**Time Keeper**:
A dying Time Keeper met early in the Lands between, before the Lesser god. The Time Keeper is also Almos's teacher: gives him his Mana bar and teaches him to channel, meaning control, breath, intent and cost. Shove, Pull and Deep bolt are what that control becomes through Almos's own Path. The Time Keeper also tells him of the lost city and its memory tree before dying.
_Avoid_: Mage teacher, mentor, the old one, the keeper

**Mani**:
A wind-casting mage met in the Tempest.
_Avoid_: The storm mage

**Cersei**:
Mani's ally, a healer, met in the Tempest.
_Avoid_: The healer

### Enemies

**Pursuit**:
An enemy chasing the player once it has noticed him. A pursuing enemy follows him through a Room's exit after a short delay. Pursuit ends at once when the player gets into a Hiding spot while out of the enemy's Sight, or after he has stayed out of its Sight, without a break, for a set time; seeing him again starts that time over. Out of its Sight, the enemy walks to the place it last saw him and waits there until the time runs out. It then returns to its post, facing the way it was set, and recovers Health at a set rate there and on the way, never in Pursuit. An attack already under way is finished first, and a hit from the player always puts it in Pursuit again.
_Avoid_: Aggro, agro, alert, hunting

**Sight**:
The cone in front of an enemy within which it can notice the player. Noticing is instant: it notices him on the first physics frame he is inside the cone with no wall between. Walls block it. The cone shrinks in darkness, its length following the light where the player stands from full light down to a short length in darkness, and a torch put out with Flame draw darkens its surroundings. An enemy at its post faces a set direction, not the player, and an enemy that is hit notices the player whatever its Sight shows.
_Avoid_: Vision (that is the dream place), line of sight, view cone, detection

**Noise**:
The sound the player makes by Sprinting, Rolling and fighting, each reaching its own distance; walking, standing, Blocking, Jumping and hiding make none. Only a Sound hunter hears it, and walls do not stop it.
_Avoid_: Sound, loudness

**Sound hunter**:
A creature that notices the player by Noise instead of Sight, so darkness does not help against it. It notices him as soon as he is within the distance of the Noise he is making, through walls, and never by Sight; for it, being in its Sight means being within that distance. In Pursuit it loses him once he has been silent, or out of earshot, for the same set time as any other enemy.
_Avoid_: Blind hunter, listener

**Hiding spot**:
Any place common sense says a person could hide, such as a barrel, under a bed or behind a curtain. The player gets in with a tap of Interact within reach and leaves with another tap. Inside he cannot move or act, makes no Noise and cannot be noticed. Getting in while out of the Sight of a pursuer ends its Pursuit at once. An enemy that sees him get in, being in its Sight on that frame (a Sound hunter: within the Noise he made the frame before, so hiding right after a Roll is heard and hiding after walking is not), goes to the Hiding spot and drags him out with an Unblockable grab, which is its Unblockable attack taken out of its pattern, with the usual long wind-up; if something stops it short of the spot, it gives up the drag as it would lose him, once the lose-time has run, from the moment it is free to head for the spot (after any attack already under way), without the grab starting, and he stays hidden. If he taps to leave during the wind-up he can Roll away from it; if he stays in, he is pulled out and takes the grab's damage. If he leaves first, Sight and Pursuit go on as usual. The test room has a barrel, a bed and a curtain as placeholders.
_Avoid_: Cover, stealth spot, hidey-hole

**Boss**:
A unique enemy fought only at a story turning point. Once killed it stays dead. The only Boss in the first areas is the Lesser god, in the Lands between.
_Avoid_: Miniboss, elite, guardian

**Named enemy**:
An enemy placed in the world as one of a kind. Once killed it stays dead, like a Boss. Every other enemy is ordinary and comes back at a rest or a Death.
_Avoid_: Elite, unique, champion

**Lesser god**:
The minor deity Almos meets in the Vision in the Lands between; the first Boss. It offers him a truth in exchange for his ability to sleep, and will not let him leave the Vision without a bargain; refusing only brings the offer back. Once he agrees and the trade is made, it still refuses to let him leave. The Boss fight is his escape from the Vision. The trade happens once; on later attempts the god greets him briefly and the fight begins. The fight is a melee duel that tests Parry, Low attacks and Unblockable attacks, bent by the dream with False strikes.
_Avoid_: Dream god, the god

**False strike**:
A dream copy of one of the Lesser god's attacks that looks real but hits nothing. Only the real strike has the true wind-up. Parrying a False strike counts as a missed Parry.
_Avoid_: Fake attack, illusion, feint

**Undead**:
A body still animated by the magical energy left in it after its mage overcast and died; the person is gone. Most stand motionless; now and then one wakes by pure chance, with no pattern, and fights. A waking Undead always gives a clear warning and takes a moment to rise before it can attack.
_Avoid_: Zombie, hollow, revenant

**Small Ilion**:
An Ilion that does not attack but hurts anyone who touches it. It can be burnt.
_Avoid_: Ilion sprout, minor Ilion

**Large Ilion**:
A rooted Ilion that cannot move but lashes out at anyone in reach with whip-like tendrils. It can be burnt or fought up close.
_Avoid_: Ilion whip, greater Ilion

**Crawler**:
A small, fast creature of the dark that hunts in packs in the Base of the tree.
_Avoid_: Bug, mite

**Nar guard**:
An armed Nar warrior with a spear who guards the arch tree; the enemy of the Capture, and of the Fugitive.
_Avoid_: Soldier, sentry

**Nar jailer**:
A Nar guard of the Dungeons who Blocks and Parries the player's attacks.
_Avoid_: Warden, prison guard

**Root crawler**:
A Sound hunter that swarms in the dark Rooms of the Dungeons.
_Avoid_: Root bug, swarm

**Branch thrower**:
A Nar who throws spears in the Upper tree from branches the player cannot reach.
_Avoid_: Archer, spearman

**Canopy flier**:
A creature of the Upper tree that swoops down on the player from above.
_Avoid_: Bat, glider

**Fungus beast**:
A slow creature of the Lands between that leaves lingering spore clouds on the ground.
_Avoid_: Mushroom monster, sporeling

**Storm flier**:
A creature of the Tempest that dives along the Wind, faster when it flies with it.
_Avoid_: Storm bird, harpy

**Necro mage**:
A hostile mage met in the Tempest who channels against the player.
_Avoid_: Necromancer, dark mage

**Unblockable attack**:
An enemy attack, such as a grab or the heaviest slam, that can be neither Blocked nor Parried; only a Roll avoids it, or a Jump that carries the player out of its reach (a Jump gives no invulnerability, so one that stays within reach is hit like any other). It always has a distinct wind-up that warns the player: a long one with a white flash at its start, a tint and a sound that no other attack uses. It goes through Block in full, spending no Stamina and never beginning a Guard break, and goes through a Parry in full: the Parry is not asked, so it is not spent and its window stays open, and the enemy is not staggered. It hits a target in the air like any attack, and is not a Low attack. The training enemy takes its attacks in a fixed repeating order set in its tuning.
_Avoid_: Perilous attack, guard breaker

**Low attack**:
An enemy attack that travels along the ground, such as a tail swipe or a shockwave, and can be cleared with a Jump. It hits only a target on the ground: one in the air as it passes is missed, like one who is untouchable, so the same swing still catches him if he comes down while it is on. Against a target on the ground it is like any attack: it can be Blocked and Parried, and a Parry builds Stagger as usual. It has a wind-up of its own with a tint of its own, and a wider strike than the enemy's plain attack so that it reads as a sweep. The training enemy takes its attacks in a fixed repeating order set in its tuning; a Break does not reset that order or skip a place in it.
_Avoid_: Sweep, ground attack
